


Business Description & Problem Statement
Royal Square Financial (Pty) Ltd is an independent full-service Brokerage and Financial Services Provider that is focused on wealth creation and wealth preservation through financial planning, offering a range of insurance and investment solutions to the public, including life insurance, health insurance, Health Insurance, Funeral Insurance, Personal Insurance, Commercial Insurance and goal-based Investments.
We’re a broker. We sell investment and insurance products from South Africa’s biggest providers like Sanlam, Old Mutual, Liberty, Momentum, Discovery, Allan Gray, Santam and around a dozen others, but the advice we give clients is our own. We’re not tied to any of them.
The problem is paperwork. Regulation means every adviser needs formal qualifications, and every client interaction generates compliance admin: identity checks, record-keeping, data protection. That load grows every year. We now spend more time on forms than with the people we’re meant to be advising, and software is the only way out.
What we need you to build
A system that makes communication between Royal Square Financial and our clients faster and less manual.
Three core pieces:
A client dashboard
A real-time view of each client’s financial position and net worth, in one place.
Automated reminders
For tasks, documents, renewals, and reviews — sent to us, to the client, or to both.
Some examples:
Insurance valuation certificates: every 2 years → us and the client
Driving licence expiry → client
Annual financial review meeting → us
Retirement fee renewal → us
Birthdays and anniversaries → us, automated
There are many more. Assume the list keeps growing.
Goal tracking
Advisers should be able to load goals for a client — individual or shared — and the dashboard should show visually how far along each one is.

Example: a full single task
Short-term insurance — reporting an accident
The client taps Report an Accident or Loss.
The app immediately sends back a checklist of what to gather at the scene:
Photos of the road surface and direction of travel
The address or nearest cross streets
Photos of all vehicles and people involved
Licence plates and registration discs
ID documents of everyone involved
Witness names and contact details, plus a voice note if possible
Insurance details of the other parties
A reminder to report to the police within 48 hours
Getting this right at the scene is what makes the claim go smoothly later.

Registering the claim
The client taps Register a Motor Claim and selects their insurer — Santam, for example, though we work with many.
The app collects:
Date, time, and description of the incident
Whether police were notified, and the case number
Witness details and whether a statement was taken
Who was driving, and whether the vehicle was in personal or business use
Details of other vehicles or property involved
Third-party details: licence, vehicle registration, insurer and policy number
And uploads to the cloud:
Photos of all vehicles, damage, road surface, registration discs and licences
Driver’s licence and a sketch of the accident

What happens after submission
This part runs over days and weeks, and the app’s job is to track it and keep everyone informed:
Insurer returns a claim number and a claims handler
Client takes the vehicle for assessment
Assessment goes to the insurer and to us
Repair quotes go to the insurer
Insurer authorises repairs
Client picks a date for the vehicle to go in
We arrange car hire and delivery to the repairer
Weekly repair updates pushed to us
We arrange collection and return of the hire car
Client writes a short review and closes the transaction

Other tasks the app should handle
Change of address
Change of bank details
Request a policy document
Request a border letter
Request an IRP5 from an investment company
Request a consultation
Client information collection
Balance sheet and income statement
One principle throughout
The more that passes straight through to the product provider automatically — API, direct integration, file transfer — the more useful the app becomes.

Slide 1 — The Problem
“Financial advisory firms like Royal Square manage clients, financial products and communication across emails, WhatsApp and manual processes. This creates unnecessary administration and makes it harder to manage clients and complete financial transactions efficiently.”
Slide 2 — Who Needs This
“Our customers are financial advisory and brokerage firms like Royal Square. They serve individuals who need financial advice and want to buy products such as insurance and investments from different financial providers. The more clients and products a brokerage manages, the harder this becomes to coordinate.”
Slide 3 — Our Solution
“We built ClientConnect, software that gives the brokerage and its clients one shared platform. Clients can see their financial position, goals and products, and chat directly with their adviser. Advisers can manage their clients, recommend products, respond to requests and approve actions. Notifications keep everyone updated without relying on scattered emails and WhatsApp messages.”
Slide 4 — The Business
“Our business model is transaction-based. Brokerage firms can use ClientConnect to manage their clients, and we earn a fee when transactions are completed through the platform. This means our revenue grows as our customers manage more clients and complete more financial transactions.”
Slide 5 — Live Demo
“I’ll start as a client. I can see my financial position, goals and insurance, and chat with my adviser when I need help. I’ll then switch to the adviser, who can see that client, respond to their message, manage their products and approve their request. The client is automatically notified when something changes.”
Slide 6 — What's Next
“Today we connect the client and adviser. Next, we want to connect financial product providers directly, allowing product information and transactions to flow through the platform. Later, other providers such as assessors and repairers can join relevant workflows, reducing the back-and-forth currently happening through email.”
